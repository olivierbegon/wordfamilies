For the grown-up

  • Keep it short: 5–10 minutes, stop while she still wants more.
  • In Part 2, have her say the first sound, then “{{F}}”, then blend them: “{{BLEND}}” Blending onset + rime is the skill word families build.
  • In Part 3, cover the words and ask her to read before tapping, so it isn't guessing by elimination.
  • Pair this with the school's booklets on Hubbard's Cupboard ({{BOOKLETS}}) and magnetic letters on the fridge.
  • Praise the effort and the sounding out, not just right answers.
